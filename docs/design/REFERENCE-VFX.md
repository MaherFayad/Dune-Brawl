# Reference visual effects

Status: IN PROGRESS. Catalog being filled from 60 fps strips of the reference footage. Frames and clips stay in the scratch folder, never in the repo.

Units: **bodies** (one fighter's standing height) and **frames at 60 fps**.

## Early findings (to be ranked)

1. **The victim turns into a flat silhouette in their own damage colour when hit.** The whole sprite is filled with one flat colour that matches the damage ring in the HUD (orange at mid damage, deep red at high damage), held for the hit pause, and it stretches into a smear along the launch direction on strong hits.
2. **Launches leave a thick, dense, cel-shaded cloud trail.** White puffs with light grey shading, flat (not glowing), packed into one continuous column 0.3 to 0.6 bodies thick that grows, holds for about 30 frames and breaks up into loose puffs by about 50 frames.
3. **KO is a big flat burst at the screen edge, not a beam across the screen.** A splash of white plus a cosmetic colour (cyan in one KO, magenta in another) with straight speed lines and little stars, then a giant cartoon cloud that fills a third of the screen height and lingers over 1 s.
4. **Off-screen bubble.** When a fighter leaves the camera, a grey translucent circle with a dark rim appears at the screen edge with the fighter drawn inside it.
