# Dana, Bride of the Sea (`sea-siren`)

Zoner. Working name in the canon roster: Sea Siren of Sindbad. The fighter id, asset folder and string keys stay `sea-siren` so nothing downstream breaks.

## 1. Name options

| # | English | Arabic | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dana** | **دانة** | In Gulf pearling speech, a dana is the great, flawless pearl, the one a whole season of diving hopes for. A real given name in the Gulf. |
| 2 | Lulwa | لولوة | The Gulf form of "pearl" and a common Gulf name. Warm, but softer on the character select screen and close in meaning to Dana with less legend behind it. |
| 3 | Bint al-Bahr | بنت البحر | "Daughter of the sea", the divers' own epithet for sea maidens. Strong folklore, but it reads as a description, not a person. |

**Recommended: Dana (دانة).** It is short (4 letters in both scripts, readable on a small select card), it is rooted in Gulf pearl-diving heritage instead of the Greek siren, it names the very thing her shell protects, and it carries none of the fish-tail baggage of "siren" or "mermaid", which helps keep the art on two feet.

**Title: Bride of the Sea (عروس البحر).** This is the Arabic sea-folklore root: divers' tales of the sea maiden who calls sailors home or away. In lore she is called this by the pearling crews; she never uses it herself. Art prompts never use the words "bride", "mermaid" or "siren", to avoid bridal gowns and fish tails in generation.

## 3. Lore

**Bio (about 240 words).**
Pearl divers tell of a girl who rose out of the oyster banks on the first morning of a diving season, when the fleet's singer, the nahham, called the dive. The crews of a small pearling village took her in and named her Dana, after the pearl every diver hopes for. She learned the rhythm of their work: the stone weight that carries a diver to the seabed, the rope that hauls him back, the long songs that keep a boat's oars together. She learned that the sea is never cruel to those who respect it, and that every boat that sails out must sail home.

She is a sea spirit of the waters Sindbad sailed, older than she looks and younger than she pretends. She remembers the tales of his voyages the way a reef remembers ships: the great bird whose kin sank a merchant ship with stones, the island that turned out to be a whale. She carries a pearl oyster shell as wide as her shoulders, and when she throws it, it returns to her hand the way the fleet returns at season's end, the homecoming the divers call al-Qaffal.

Now the storm above the Empty Quarter is drinking the sea into the sky. The oyster banks are thinning, the boats wait in harbor, and the village has stopped singing. Dana followed a waterspout up into the Drift to stand in the calm ring on the Night of Suhail.

**Her claim.** That every drop the storm has drunk be returned to the Gulf, and the oyster banks with it, so the fleet can sail out and come home again.

**Relationships.**
- **Djinn Warrior:** She likes the swordsman and does not trust the voice in his sash; she has heard many bargains made over water and few kept.
- **Ghoul of the Dunes:** It lured caravans to be lost; she calls travelers home. She treats it like a rival in the same trade and scolds it like a neighbor.
- **Falcon Rider (rival):** The sea remembers that the great bird's kin sank ships, and the storm grants only one claim: the rider wants the cliffs brought down, she wants the sea brought back, and neither will wait another year.
- **Dagger Dancer:** Two clever women who win by timing; Dana admires her and challenges her to keep rhythm, song against step.

**Victory line.**
- EN: "Every wave comes home. Tonight, so does the sea."
- AR: "كل موجة تعود إلى شاطئها، والليلة يعود البحر."

**Taunt line.**
- EN: "Hold your breath. You are in my water now."
- AR: "احبس أنفاسك، فأنت الآن في مائي."

**Why she reads differently as a black shape.**
- The Djinn Warrior is a tall vertical turban plus a long curved blade line and trailing sash.
- The Ghoul of the Dunes is a wide, hunched, low-headed mass with huge claws.
- The Falcon Rider is a long diagonal spear plus the big wing shapes of the falcon.
- The Dagger Dancer is a slim, low, twisting shape with flaring dance cloth and two small blades.
- Dana is the only upright figure with a **large circle on her back** and a **forward hook at the head**, and she carries no long weapon line at all.

Her silhouette also changes in play: while the shell is flying, the circle is gone from her back. That empty back tells the opponent that her side heavy is unavailable, which is the main approach window (section 6).

## 6. Hook: the Homecoming Shell (محارة القفال)

Al-Qaffal is the homecoming of the pearling fleet at the end of the season. Her shell is one projectile that goes out, hangs, and comes home.

### Inputs

| Input (ground or air) | Shell state | Result |
|---|---|---|
| B or Side + B | In hand, cooldown 0 | **Shell Cast** (side heavy, move 5): melee release sweep plus the shell spawns. |
| B or Side + B | Out (flying out or hovering) | **Call (نداء العودة)**: hook action, 6/1/12 frames, no hitbox. On its frame 6 the shell switches straight to its return leg. |
| B or Side + B | Returning, adrift, or in hand with cooldown above 0 | Nothing. The press sits in the normal 7-frame buffer and fires if the shell becomes available in time. |
| Down + B, Up + B, any A | Any | Unchanged: Tidal Ring on the ground, Stone Dive (ground pound) with Down + B in the air, Diver's Ascent, lights. |
| B in the air | As above | Plain B in the air is the side heavy slot (canon section 6), so Shell Cast and Call work in the air exactly as on the ground. Shell Cast is not an `air` move: landing during it does not cancel it. |

### Integer state fields (added to the fighter record)

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sh` | 0 in hand, 1 out, 2 hover, 3 return, 4 adrift |
| `shT` | Frames in the current state |
| `shX`, `shY` | Shell center, sim units |
| `shVX`, `shVY` | Shell velocity, sim units per frame |
| `shF` | Facing at the moment of the throw (+1 or -1) |
| `shHit` | Bitmask: 1 = outbound leg spent, 2 = return leg spent |
| `shCd` | Frames before Shell Cast is allowed again (counts down only while `sh` = 0) |
| `shLife` | Frames since spawn while in states 1 to 3 |

### Rules (all values from `hook` and `projectile` in moves.draft.json)

1. **Spawn.** Shell Cast can start only when `sh = 0` and `shCd = 0`. On frame 13 (`spawnFrame`, the first active frame) the shell spawns at `x + face * 36 px`, `y - 36 px` (chest height) with `shVX = face * 1800`, `shVY = 0`, `shF = face`, `sh = 1`. If Dana is hit before frame 13, no shell spawns. Only one shell per fighter can exist (`maxOnScreen` 1), enforced by the `sh = 0` check.
2. **Outbound (`sh = 1`).** Each frame: `shX += shVX`, then `shVX -= shF * 75`. After exactly 24 frames `shVX` reaches 0: set `sh = 2`. Travel is 22,500 units (225 px), so the apex sits 261 px from where she stood. Flat path, no vertical motion.
3. **Hover (`sh = 2`).** The shell spins in place for 10 frames with its outbound hitbox still live (if unspent), then `sh = 3`, `shVX = 0`.
4. **Return (`sh = 3`).** Target is Dana's chest: `tx = x`, `ty = y - 3600`. Each frame: `shVX += sign(tx - shX) * 100`, clamped to plus or minus 2000; `shX += shVX`; `shVY = clamp(ty - shY, -300, 300)`; `shY += shVY`. It follows her horizontally with real acceleration but climbs or drops at most 3 px per frame.
5. **Catch.** After the return move, if `|shX - x| <= 2800` and `|shY - ty| <= 4000` and Dana is not in hitstun: `sh = 0`, all shell fields reset, `shCd = 24`. Catching is automatic and never locks her state; it is allowed during her attacks and dodges. The 56 px catch window is wider than the 20 px maximum step, so the shell can never skip past her.
6. **Lifetime.** If `shLife` reaches 150 frames before a catch (for example she is high above it), the shell bursts into foam: `sh = 0`, `shCd = 60`.
7. **Hit while it is out.** If Dana enters hitstun while `sh` is 1, 2 or 3: `sh = 4` (adrift), `shT = 0`, `shVX = 0`, `shVY = 150`. Adrift has no hitbox and sinks 1.5 px per frame for 40 frames, then `sh = 0`, `shCd = 50`. Total: 90 frames with no Shell Cast.
8. **Death.** When Dana enters the dead state, every shell field resets to 0 (shell in hand, no cooldown) for the respawn drop.
9. **Hitbox.** A 24 x 24 px box centered on the shell (`projectile.box`), tested against the opponent's hurtbox like a melee box. Each leg hits at most once:
   - **Outbound and hover leg:** dmg 6, kb 300, kg 14, launch `(shF * 85, -53)`. Pushes away and slightly up. Hitstun 12 frames at 0%, 15 at 100%.
   - **Return leg:** dmg 4, kb 340, kg 6, launch `(sign(shVX) * 70, -71)`: along the shell's travel, which is toward Dana. If `shVX` is 0 it uses `-shF`. Low growth so it never kills; it drags. Hitstun 13 frames at 0%, 14 at 100%.
   - The shell skips a target that is dead, invulnerable (`inv > 0`), or in the invulnerable part of a dodge, without spending the leg.
   - If Shell Cast's own melee sweep connects, the outbound leg is marked spent immediately, so a point-blank cast never double-hits on the way out.
   - Shell hits use the same hit reaction as melee (state HIT, hitstun `6 + floor(l / 48)` after weight, recovery refreshed, at least 1 jump given back) and set hit pause `3 + floor(dmg / 3)`, which freezes the shell too.
10. **Opponent's attacks (swat).** If any active hitbox of the opponent overlaps the shell box while `sh` is 1, 2 or 3, the shell is swatted: `shHit = 3` (both legs spent) and, if it was outbound or hovering, `sh = 3`, `shVX = 0`. It flies home harmless and is caught normally. Swatting does not use up the opponent's attack, which can still hit Dana. A thrown weapon (phase 4) swats it the same way.
11. **Two shells (mirror match).** If two shells in states 1 to 3 overlap, both are swatted.
12. **Stage edges and terrain.** The shell ignores soft platforms. If an outbound or hovering shell's center enters solid terrain (the main platform block, or a Hejaz Ruins wall), it turns back at once (`sh = 3`, `shVX = 0`). A returning shell ignores terrain so it can never get stuck. If the shell center leaves the blast zone rectangle, it turns back the same way. On today's Dune Island layout the apex from the ledge is about 520 px short of the side blast line, so this is only a safety rule.

### Frame timeline (Dana standing still, nothing in the way)

| Frame | Event |
|---|---|
| 0 | Shell Cast pressed (13-frame held wind-up, shell drawn back behind her head) |
| 13 to 15 | Melee release sweep active; shell spawns on 13 |
| 37 | Apex, 261 px away |
| 38 | Dana can act again (side heavy total 38 frames) |
| 37 to 47 | Hover |
| about 69 | Caught |
| about 93 | Shell Cast allowed again (24-frame cooldown) |

Measured with a reference model of these rules on top of the prototype sim: apex 261 px, catch on frame 70 counting the press as frame 1.

### Counterplay (the approach windows)

1. **Read the wind-up.** 13 frames of startup with a big held pose. Jump or dodge on sight.
2. **One lane.** The shell flies flat at her chest height. A short hop clears it, and the return leg only climbs 3 px per frame, so a jump usually clears it again.
3. **Empty back, no side heavy.** From frame 38 to about frame 93, her neutral B is only Call, a 19-frame commitment with no hitbox. Rush in during this window.
4. **Swat it.** Any attack that meets the shell sends it home harmless. The opponent's attack is not used up.
5. **Hit her while it is out.** She loses the shell for 90 frames, the biggest punish in her kit.
6. **Punish an aerial cast from below.** In the air the wind-up is the same 13 frames while she drifts slowly down (gravity 88%, fall speed 90%), so an aerial Shell Cast is easy to read and to punish from underneath.

### Why it is rollback safe

All 10 fields are integers on the fighter record, so they are covered by the existing clone and hash (both iterate the fighter's key list). The update uses only addition, `sign`, `abs`, clamps and the existing truncating division for knockback: no floats, no square roots, no trig, no random numbers, no clock reads. Every constant comes from the hashed moves.json. The update order is fixed (section 12), so both peers resolve simultaneous events identically. The renderer only reads `sh`, `shX`, `shY` and `shLife` (spin angle is `shLife * 36` degrees, render only), and the catch and hit sounds use the plan's 2-frame rollback dedupe.

Rules bent, and why:
- **Damage outside the 10 slots.** The shell adds up to 10 damage per throw (6 out, 4 back) that the slot sum does not count. That is why the slot sum sits at 92 instead of the baseline 99: her melee is weaker so her total output matches the others only when she lands the shell.
- **Side heavy has two parts.** The K of 1080 belongs to the point-blank release sweep only. The shell itself has a K of 440 (out) and 400 (return) and cannot kill. K sits 60 below the all-rounder because the same button also throws.
- **An 11th action.** Call is not a slot; it borrows the side heavy input only while the shell is out. Listed under canon changes.

Kill estimates (prototype units, before the global retune multiplier, against weight 100). Anchoring to the plan's target that the baseline side heavy kills from the ledge at about 125% (launch strength about 1285): Shell Cast kills from the ledge at about 137%, Tidal Ring at about 155%. She kills late; the shell is how she gets them there.

## 8. Game plan

Hitstun is `6 + floor(l / 48)`. Ranges below were checked in a scratch harness running the live prototype sim with these moves and a reference model of the shell, against a weight 100 opponent with no directional influence. The planned 3.5x knockback multiplier and phase 2 directional influence will move these ranges; retest after the retune.

### Bread and butter

1. **Undertow into Wave Crest** (down light, up light). Undertow lifts them straight up and slightly toward her. True combo from about **40%** upward (at 40% the launch is 484, hitstun 16, which covers her 12 remaining frames plus Wave Crest's 4 startup). Below 40% there is a 1 to 2 frame gap that only a fast dodge escapes. 14 damage.
2. **Homecoming into Wave Crest** (return leg, up light). The return leg drags them in with 13 to 14 frames of hitstun at any percent; Wave Crest's 4-frame startup leaves about 9 frames plus the 4-frame hit pause to confirm it. Works from **0% to about 100%**, after which they land outside the arc. 12 damage plus the 6 from the outbound hit if it landed earlier.
3. **The tide lane (zoning pattern).** Shell Cast at mid range, then hold position. A grounded opponent eats the outbound or hover hit (pushed away, 12 to 15 frames of hitstun, not enough to follow). An opponent who short hops the shell lands in front of her and meets Wave Crest or Foam Palm, and the returning shell then catches them from behind on the way home. The outbound hit into the return hit is **not** a true combo (about 30 frames apart); it is a guessing game, which keeps it fair. Use Spray Kick and Foam Palm to push anyone who gets close back out to shell range, then cast again when the cooldown ends.

### Kill setups

1. **The pincer: Homecoming into Tidal Ring.** Throw, then let the opponent advance between Dana and the returning shell. Start Tidal Ring so its active frames meet the shell's arrival: the return hit (hitstun 13 to 14) is a true link into Tidal Ring's 12-frame startup, with only 1 to 2 frames to spare, so it is a timed setup, not a reaction. In the harness it combos at every percent tested (40% to 140%); Tidal Ring's launch is 1095 at 120% and 1191 at 140%, a kill near the ledge from about 150%.
2. **The ledge net.** With the opponent offstage, cast from the ledge (or from a short hop to raise the lane): the shell lane sits at ledge height and hangs at its apex for 10 frames, so the low route back is covered. If they go high, jump out and Plunge Kick (spike, `ay` 95) to send them down, then cover the ledge with Tidal Ring if they make it back. The spike kills offstage from about 90%, and Shell Cast's melee sweep kills on a ledge landing from about 137%.

### Playing against her

- Watch her back. No shell on her back means no side heavy for about a second: that is your time to rush.
- Jump the wind-up, not the shell. Her 13-frame held pose is the tell; a short hop clears the chest-height lane.
- Swat the shell with any attack when it hangs at the apex: it goes home harmless.
- Do not stand between her and the returning shell near 100%+. Move up or past her instead.
- Hit her while the shell is out and she is unarmed for 90 frames. Stay on her: she is floaty and light, easy to juggle and early to kill.
- When she casts from a jump, get under her: she falls slowly through 38 frames of commitment.

## 9. Matchups

- **Djinn Warrior:** Even. His sand dash closes the gap fast, so cast only at full range and keep Tidal Ring ready for the end of the dash.
- **Ghoul of the Dunes:** Favors Dana. He is slow and big, so the shell lands often, but never cast into his armored heavy at close range: armor absorbs the sweep and he punishes the 22 recovery frames.
- **Falcon Rider (rival):** Slightly unfavorable. The extra jump flies over any single lane, and an aerial cast to meet the rider leaves floaty Dana exposed, so she plays under the falcon with Wave Crest and saves the shell for the moments the rider must land.
- **Dagger Dancer:** Slightly unfavorable. The rushdown dodges the wind-up and punishes the empty back; Dana wins by keeping Foam Palm and Spray Kick in front of her and throwing only after a push.
